the pricing models for the extension are as follows:

- Free: $0/month
- Pro: $10/month
- Team: $20/month

the free plan will have the following features:

- 3 trees in history
- no sync across devices
- local-only data
- no online features

the pro plan will have the following features:

- unlimited trees in history
- sync across devices
- 10 devices per account
- online features
- premium support
- shareable trails

the team plan will have the following features:

- unlimited trees in history
- sync across devices and teams
- online features
- shared workspaces
- team analytics
- admin controls
