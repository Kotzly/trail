# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: January 29, 2026_

## 1. Introduction
Trail ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Trail browser extension, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- Account information (if applicable): email address, username
- Feedback and support requests

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- Browsing trails and navigation steps you explicitly choose to record
- Extension settings and preferences
- Basic technical data (browser type, extension version)

We **do not** collect passwords, payment card details, or sensitive personal data by default.

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Full browsing history outside recorded trails
- Keystrokes, form inputs, or passwords
- Data from private/incognito sessions (unless explicitly enabled by you)

## 3. How We Use Information
We use the collected information to:
- Provide and operate the Service
- Store and display your recorded trails
- Improve product functionality and usability
- Provide customer support
- Ensure security and prevent abuse

## 4. Data Storage and Security
- Recorded trails are stored securely using industry-standard safeguards
- We apply access controls and encryption where appropriate
- No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data

## 5. Data Sharing
We do **not** sell your data.

We may share data only:
- With service providers strictly necessary to operate the Service
- If required by law or valid legal process
- To protect the rights, safety, and integrity of Trail

## 6. Data Retention
- Trails are stored until you delete them
- Account-related data is retained while your account is active
- You may delete your data at any time through the Service

## 7. Your Rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data
- Object to or restrict processing

To exercise your rights, contact us at: **support@gettrail.net**

## 8. Children’s Privacy
Trail is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with a new “Last updated” date.
